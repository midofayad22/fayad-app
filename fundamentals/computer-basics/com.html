1. Evolution & History of Computers

Computers have evolved from massive, room-sized calculating machines into sleek, powerful devices that fit in our pockets. The journey spans from mechanical calculators like Babbage's Difference Engine to vacuum tubes, transistors, integrated circuits, and modern microprocessors.

  • 1940s: First Generation (Vacuum Tubes - ENIAC)
  • 1950s: Second Generation (Transistors)
  • 1960s: Third Generation (Integrated Circuits)
  • 1970s-Present: Fourth & Fifth Generations (Microprocessors & AI)

2. Hardware vs. Software

A computer system is fundamentally divided into two primary components that work hand-in-hand:

Hardware

The physical, tangible parts of the computer such as the CPU, RAM, Motherboard, Hard Drive, and peripheral devices.

Software

The intangible instructions, operating systems, and programs (like Windows, Linux, and applications) that tell the hardware what to do.
