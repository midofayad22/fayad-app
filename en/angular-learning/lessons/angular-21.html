  3. Which statement best describes this topic?

    • Angular provides template-driven and reactive forms. Forms allow developers to collect input, track control state, validate values, and respond to submission.
    • Validation ensures that submitted data follows rules such as required fields, minimum length, maximum length, email format, and custom business rules. Angular exposes control states such as valid, invalid, touched, dirty, and pending.
    • HttpClient is Angular's standard tool for making HTTP requests. It can perform GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, and DELETE requests and works naturally with Observables.
