## Decisions each level tests

- **1. First Shot** — Two zombies shamble in from both sides. Write act so it shoots the zombie when it is in a straight line, otherwise waits.
- **2. Running Dry** — Only two rounds in the magazine. Reload when it runs dry and melee when a zombie gets next to you.
- **3. Fastest First** — Runners move every turn. If you only shoot the nearest zombie you will get bitten. Pick targets by type, not by distance.
- **4. Behind the Barricade** — Barricades stop bullets but break. Shoot only zombies with a clear line and do not waste ammo.
- **5. The Medkit** — You start with only 2 hp. Reach the medkit in the corner, then heal before the zombies arrive.
- **6. Escort** — Get the survivor to the exit E. Do not outrun them and do not let a zombie reach them.
- **7. The Distraction** — No weapon. Two runners guard the road. Throw noise north at the right moment, then run for the exit.
- **8. Trap Alley** — Only two rounds! The spike traps in the gaps kill whoever steps on them. Let the map work and shoot only when needed.
- **9. The Brute** — The brute has 4 hp and bites for 2. Back away whenever it gets close and shoot while it follows in line.
- **10. Spitters** — A spitter hurts you from 3 cells away when you share a line. Line up first and shoot from range 4.
- **11. Ammo Budget** — Four rounds, more zombies than that. Spend one round on each and finish the rest with the knife.
- **12. Hold Out** — Waves keep coming. You do not have to kill everyone, only survive 20 turns. Reload between waves.
- **13. Evacuation** — Two survivors are behind you. Shoot every threat before it reaches them, then move to the exit once they catch up.
- **14. Ghost** — Three zombies roam the road and you have two noise makers. Throw the first at the start, the second halfway.
- **15. Final Stand** — A brute and two spitters behind spike traps, two runners coming from behind. Use the map, medkit and ammo wisely.

## Reading the table

- Levels 1, 2, 4 are tutorials: the nearest-first fighter also wins, which is intended (they teach the basic loop).
- Levels 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, 14 cannot be won by the generic nearest-first fighter: they require type priorities, healing, escorting, noise timing, kiting, ammo budgeting or protecting allies. Several are also winnable by *other* tailored strategies (e.g. the kiter on 3, 5, 9), which is intended: more than one valid approach.
- Every level has more than one valid solution shape (different target order, different reload timing, melee vs ranged); the star bonus rewards efficiency.
